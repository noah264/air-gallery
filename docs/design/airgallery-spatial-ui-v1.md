# AirGallery UI concept v1

Generation mode: built-in image_gen.

Design: spatial photo gallery with curved photo wall, selected photo, local directory control and gesture feedback.

Image: [airgallery-spatial-ui-v1.png](./airgallery-spatial-ui-v1.png)

This image is a visual concept, not a working application. Photo titles and counts are sample content. The final design should preserve photo geometry and allow gesture guidance and camera preview to collapse.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a polished, high-fidelity desktop app UI concept screenshot for "AirGallery", a local photo browser controlled with webcam hand gestures, for a Chinese-speaking user. This is the actual flat app screen, not a photo of a monitor, not a marketing landing page. Wide landscape 16:10 composition, crisp generous typography and precise UI components. The experience must clearly have three-dimensional spatial depth.

Visual direction: elegant futuristic spatial photography gallery, restrained and premium. Near-black midnight navy background, subtle atmospheric blue illumination toward center, fine translucent panels, tiny ice-blue highlights. Photos themselves provide rich natural colors. No excessive neon, no sci-fi cockpit, no technical overlays.

Layout:
1. Thin top navigation bar: left a small geometric aperture symbol and "AirGallery"; adjacent small caption "空间相册". Right a rounded local-directory chip with folder icon and "Pictures / Travel", then a small button "更换目录", fullscreen icon and settings icon.
2. Very spacious immersive main canvas occupying about 75% of height. A gently curved panorama of roughly 12 frameless photographic cards in two staggered rows, angled naturally toward a central vanishing area. Cards nearer the edges recede in depth and size with tasteful dimming. Important: this must look like a real usable interactive Three.js gallery rendered inside an app, not a physical museum with walls or furniture. Show varied beautiful editorial travel photos: alpine turquoise lake, desert dunes, green forest, Iceland waterfall, seacoast cliffs, abstract architecture. Center is a larger selected landscape photograph of a luminous alpine lake and snowy mountains, almost front-facing, lifted toward the viewer from the arc. A subtle narrow icy-blue border identifies selection. Enough black negative space between photo cards; pictures remain sharp, no thick frames. Small annotation near central photograph: "阿尔卑斯 · 清晨" and below "018 / 128". Soft restrained contact glows beneath cards imply depth without a shiny floor.
3. A small hollow cyan hand-controlled cursor near right edge of the selected photo, with short caption "捏合查看". It feels like a UI cursor, not a giant hand.
4. Bottom center: a compact floating translucent toolbar with 3 separated icon/text items: "挥手翻页", "双手缩放", "捏合拖动"; below or alongside a subtle dot pagination indicator and chevrons. The guidance should feel easy to learn, not busy.
5. Lower left a tiny green dot and text "手势控制已开启", plus smaller "本机目录 · 128 张照片". Lower right a small 150px-wide webcam preview tile showing a naturally lit real hand raised for tracking, with very subtle fine hand landmarks, and a small toggle labeled "摄像头". Keep this preview subordinate and out of the main photos.
6. Place a discreet mode switch above footer reading "空间视图" selected and "网格视图" inactive.

Typography: sophisticated modern sans-serif with correct concise simplified Chinese labels and legible white / cool grey text. Consistent alignment, adequate contrast, refined 1px borders. The design is implementable for macOS, Windows and web, no OS-specific window chrome. It should communicate immersion, depth, local photo browsing, and hand-gesture control at a glance.
Avoid: browser address bars, product presentation boards, explanatory arrows, extraneous branding, huge headings, sidebars, dashboards, charts, file upload dropzones, code, debug text, futuristic hologram clutter, people standing inside gallery, watermarks. Render a finished full-screen UI design image.
