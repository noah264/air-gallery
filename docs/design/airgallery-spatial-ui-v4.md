## Design intent

- Enlarge the selected photo group at the center and leave clear negative space around its entire stack.
- Neighboring groups give way rather than overlapping or touching the selected group.
- Retain the overall elliptical spread, outward-increasing spacing, stacked photo groups and image-only home screen.
- The dense-center rule yields to the selected group's clearance margin while focused.
- This is a static visual concept; responsive sizing and actual clearance must be verified in the interactive prototype.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Edit the attached AirGallery UI v3 screenshot. Preserve the photo-only near-black background, horizontal elliptical distribution, irregular spatial photo groups, and stacked multi-page photo styling.

USER'S PRECISE CHANGE: the CURRENTLY SELECTED PHOTO GROUP at the exact CENTER must be SIGNIFICANTLY LARGER and surrounded by a CLEAR EMPTY MARGIN on all four sides. This selected group is the focal point. The surrounding groups must not overlap it or touch its edges.

Composition:
- Place ONE selected photograph group at exact screen center. Use a beautiful alpine turquoise lake and snowy mountain cover in landscape orientation. Make this center group's cover about 26% of the full canvas width, approximately 2.5 to 3 times the width of ordinary nearby groups. Keep a few thin offset photographic sheets behind the cover so it still reads as a multi-page photo group.
- The selected cover is fully visible, crisp, near front-facing, minimally rotated, visibly in front of the rest of the scene. A subtle natural lift and shadow may distinguish it. No selection border, no colored glow or icons.
- Reserve a clearly visible near-black empty buffer around the FULL EXTENT of the selected stack, including its back sheets. The gap from this stack to any other photo group should be roughly 4% of canvas width, on all sides. This negative-space breathing room is critical. No photo corners intrude into the buffer.
- Reposition the other roughly 28 to 34 photo stacks to accommodate this center selection. Smaller groups sit relatively closely outside the center buffer; farther from the middle they become increasingly widely separated. They still collectively form one horizontal elliptical cloud, with organic irregular tilt, depth and scale. Avoid neat concentric rings or symmetrical rows.
- Keep the ellipse filled by the large selected center group: this is NOT a hollow donut. Maintain lots of tiny-to-medium stacks near the central region OUTSIDE the buffer, and sparse isolated stacks at the periphery. Center enlargement must not push every other photo off-screen.
- Each group uses a first-photo cover with 3 to 5 thin photographic sheets subtly offset behind, consistent with v3. Diverse photographic covers: forest, sea cliff, desert, city night, autumn lake, architecture, coastal village. Natural colors, fine pale photo edges.
- The selection is large but leaves enough surrounding photo groups visible to perceive the whole elliptical spatial layout. Maintain generous dark outer margins.

Screen is a finished full-screen widescreen 16:10 application HOME SCREEN concept. ONLY photo groups on a near-black charcoal backdrop. ZERO text, ZERO toolbar, no logos, buttons, page counters, camera tiles, cursors, hands, orbit lines, arrows, labels, glowing circles or frames. Not a poster, no monitor, no room, no tabletop. Do not change the overall visual style into something else. Primary change is enlarged central selection with an unmistakable clear gap to its neighbors.
